Good to know

A few helpful details.

Have another question? Contact the team directly and they can help you plan.

What are the room prices?

The prices supplied by the guesthouse are P400 for a standard room and P550 for an executive room. Confirm dates, room details and availability directly.

How much does swimming cost?

Swimming is P50 per individual, according to the information provided by the guesthouse.

Can I hire the venue for an event?

Venue-only hire is P2,500. The whole place for a party is P5,000. Confirm what each option includes, capacity, timing and any house rules before booking.

How do I secure a reservation?

The guesthouse says a 50% refundable deposit is required, subject to some conditions. Contact the team to confirm the exact deposit and refund terms for your booking.

Can I bring food and drinks?

The guesthouse says food and beverages are provided only on its premises. Contact them to confirm available options and applicable arrangements.
